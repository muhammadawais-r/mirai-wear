🇵🇰 Our Story

Wear The Future — 未来を着る

Mirai Wear started with a simple frustration: Pakistan's anime, gaming and pop-culture fans had nowhere local to get apparel that actually felt premium. Imported prints were expensive and slow, local ones faded after a wash. So we built our own — 180+ GSM combed cotton, ultra-HD prints that hold up, and a store that speaks the language of the culture instead of just slapping a character on a shirt. Every piece is designed, printed and shipped from Pakistan, with Cash on Delivery so you never have to pay before you see it. This is a store built by fans, for fans — wear what you're into.
